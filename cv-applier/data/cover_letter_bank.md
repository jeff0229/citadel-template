# Cover letter bank

Stock middle paragraphs, so each application is a swap rather than a rewrite. Keep the sector paragraph a blank slot: it is researched fresh for every application.

## Opening (written fresh each time)
Name the role and the employer in the first sentence, with one specific, evidenced reason for choosing them.

## Proof paragraphs (pick one, at most two)
### Delivery
### Research
### Tools and automation

## Sector paragraph (blank slot)
A live pressure in this sector this year, from a fresh source, never the company website.

## Close
What happens next.
