# Dissertation Craft and Methodology Reference Library

A reusable, topic-neutral reference pack for a qualitative taught-MSc dissertation. Each page distils the generalisable craft from a standard UK MSc dissertation-support source, with the original project-specific examples stripped out. Institution-specific figures (word counts, deadlines, pass thresholds, ethics risk labels, named guideline bodies) are flagged throughout as items to verify against your own programme handbook. Nothing here is tied to any one topic; replace the generic placeholders with your own.

## How to use this pack

- Start with **Dissertation Structural Model (Consolidated)** as your working drafting reference.
- Drop into the per-topic pages below for precision on a specific chapter or task.
- Treat the mandatory references as compliance and structure, the advisory references as craft guidance, and the exemplar references as prose templates.

## Structure and argument

- [[Dissertation Structure and Argument]] — Six argument structures, three argument levels, chapter opening/closing test, signposting, quotation and referencing rules.
- [[Dissertation Structural Model (Consolidated)]] — Chapter-by-chapter framing that consolidates the whole pack into one drafting reference, with a verification checklist.
- [[Argumentation and Critical Thinking]] — The argumentative-versus-expository test, the (a) to (f) checklist, counter-argument framing, the "golden thread".
- [[Aims and Research Questions]] — Aim-first formulation, SMART, the aim-verb list, RQ-stem templates, the RQs grid, MSc-scale outcomes.
